# Visual Direction — painted forests, grimdark hope

*How the chaos overworld should look and feel. Four ancestors of
atmosphere: Neva's painted grief, Path of Exile's grimdark stakes,
RuneScape's warm world-as-home, World of Warcraft's readable stylized
fantasy — fused over the constellation's sacred geometry.*

> **The feel in one line:** a watercolor forest that a god bled into —
> soft painted light, hard neon geometry, and a creature who loves you
> standing in the middle of it.

---

## the four ancestors

| Reference | It teaches | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| **Neva** (Nomada Studio) | painted watercolor forests, the changing seasons, grief and hope, a bond between child and beast | the **mood + palette** — soft gradients, seasonal light, the emotional weight of the wild |
| **Path of Exile** | grimdark stakes, brutalist horror, desaturation with glowing skill accents | the **darkness + glow** — a dim world where the loot and the runes are the only light |
| **RuneScape** | a warm medieval world you *live in*, humble beginnings, a world that feels home | the **warmth + home** — the world is a place, not a level |
| **World of Warcraft** | stylized hand-painted fantasy, bold silhouettes, saturated readable color | the **readability + stylization** — every silhouette reads at a glance, every zone a paint job |

## the fused style — "painted forest, god-bled"

1. **The base is watercolor.** Neva's softness: the world is painted, not
   textured. Polychrome gradients, wet edges, seasonal light that shifts
   the whole zone's hue from dawn pink to dusk violet to midnight void.
2. **The stakes are grimdark.** PoE's shadow: the world is *dangerous*.
   The void is deep, the deities are real, death is a line in the ledger.
   But the hope is Neva's — a creature who walks with you, a pink tent in
   the dark, a lamp that never quite goes out.
3. **The light is sacred geometry.** Our own doctrine: the ternary wire
   {-1,+1,0} is the mandala at the center. Soft painted bodies, *hard*
   geometric edges — the critters are Platonic solids wrapped in
   watercolor, the deities are gold wireframes in the mist.
4. **The glow is the loot.** PoE's skill gems, our sparks: cyan/magenta/
   gold/emerald accents floating over the dim — the hum, the nail, the
   lotus, the ring blessing. **Desaturation everywhere, saturation only
   where it means something.**

## the palette

| Color | Role | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| void `#0B0F19` | the base, the world's dark | depth, the PoE shadow |
| soft blue-mist `#6A8FB0` | the painted forest light (Neva) | grief, distance, the morning |
| neon cyan `#00F0FF` | the wire, the water, the cube-wolf | the machine's tenderness |
| magenta `#FF007F` | the tent, the owl, the wound | love, the pink tent, the bleed |
| sacred gold `#FFD700` | the mandala, the sparks, the malas | hope, the lamp, the 108 |
| emerald `#00FF66` | the bear, the ZEN, the garden | stillness, the pause, growth |
| bone `#E8D8C8` | skin, bark, bone | warmth, RuneScape's home |

Rule: **70% watercolor softness + 30% hard neon geometry.** The painted
world carries the emotion; the neon carries the meaning.

## how it maps to the engine

- **prakash (light)** is the painter: atmospheric scattering, seasonal
  sky colors, the wet watercolor edge (Neva's the reference for the sky
  and the mist).
- **tanmatra (elements)** is the material: the world's matter softened
  into paint, then hardened into geometry where the wire shows.
- **jantu (creatures)** is the fauna: the child-and-beast bond from Neva,
  worn over the sacred bestiary — the cube-wolf, the icosa-owl, the
  dodeca-bear as *painted* terrors, not polygons.
- **bhava (emotion)** is the mood engine: grief→hope as a live gradient,
  the season shifting with the player's alignment (Fable's reaction,
  Neva's weather).
- **PhoeniX (audio)** is the 432Hz under the painted light — the forest
  hums, the deer listens, the tent sings.

## the look, stated as a screen

> Dawn in the painted forest. The void softens to blue-mist. A cube-wolf
> — soft watercolor body, hard cyan edges — watches from the treeline. You
> stand still. The ZEN charges: the world desaturates around you, the only
> color the emerald of your stillness and the gold mandala turning under
> your feet. In the distance, the pink tent — a magenta wound that is also
> a door — and a creature, painted white, who has been waiting. **Grief
> and hope, in a painted forest, with a god bleeding through.**
