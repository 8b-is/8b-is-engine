## the two memories, tightened

The consciousness question — *collective memory versus the individual* —
is not a philosophical boundary in this engine; it is a structural one,
compiled in `world-core::memory`:

| | **IndividualMemory** | **CollectiveMemory** |
|---|---|---|
| what it is | an actor's own projection of the ledger — the path it attested, tick by tick | the ledger H itself — every attested trace plus every durable refusal |
| carries | admitted states ($h,r,s$ along its path) | admissions **and** refusals (the world's constraints, everyone's paths) |
| can it forget refusals? | yes — they are not its path; it incurred them, it does not carry them | no — refusals are the wall's historical memory, durable, never erased |
| re-derivable | re-folded from the ledger on demand | the ledger, restart-safe (mmap) |

The executable truth (pinned by tests): a player who was refused twice has
`path_len() == 1` in its individual memory but `refusals == 2` in the
collective. **The individual remembers where it stands; the world remembers
what it refused.** That is the tight version of "consciousness mem vs
individual" — the population has memory; no single execution is the
population.

## Phoenix: mq-8b-is (the return lane)

**Phoenix** is the engine's resurrection lane, named after the oldest
strategy the theory knows: *the process dies, the strategy returns*
(Inscription Before Collusion). It is the mesh-node's restart-recovery,
compiled:

- the ledger carries the strategy — every adjudication, with its outcome;
- on boot, the world **re-folds**: `fold(seed, H) = M`, the keeper rebuilt
  from the log, the arena materialized, the applied map empty;
- **the world's clock continues where the ledger left off** — the cast's
  new ticks are continuations, never replays (the Phoenix fix: refusals
  would eat a restarting world; continuity makes the return clean).

The pantheon's own apostle: Mahākāla is the first hero *and* the last boss
(the shadow of the first hero) — the deity who returns. That is Phoenix in
game form: the process dies, the strategy returns, and the tent is pitched
again from the seed.
