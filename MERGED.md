# the convocation — what was merged into the engine

The constellation gathers its surfaces into one body. This is the story,
one sentence per door:

- **music.vaked.dev** — the live radio; GAIA at the origo, the JASMINE
  hologram, the constellation shell, the lovetta lane. Its git home
  stays `peterlodri-sec/music.vaked.dev`; this copy is the pocket mirror.
- **enthea** — the engine door (Al-Biruni measured); its git home stays
  `peterlodri-sec/enthea`; this is the doorway into this body.
- **entheai** — the bigger sibling, the manifest, the radio assets, the
  crates; home stays its own repo; the pocket copy rides here.
- **6502-is** — a 3d visual6502.org (visual6502, in three dimensions): the
  engine of the engine, the 8-bit seed everything here decodes from —
  merged in from `8b-is/6502-is` (vite + simulation, 8.5MB).

Bulk excluded at merge: `.git`, `.crush`, `node_modules`, `target`,
`dist`, `.venv`, `__pycache__`, `.wrangler`, `.benchmark` — the build
junk and the runtime memory stay where they belong; the Cargo workspace
excludes these dirs so `cargo test --workspace` stays quiet.

*one grammar, two embeddings, three doors, one engine. the constellation
· 0 + 1 · fine touch from within · vaked.dev*
