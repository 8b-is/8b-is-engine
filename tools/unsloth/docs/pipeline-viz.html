the V4.1 knob lane — big sister, wired

N-grams

N=5 is free — the shipped 384M-row engram tables fit the 5-gram buckets (128M rows of headroom, proven); N=6 would need ~16M rows per layer and is refused before any run. The remaining work is the upscaled wkv projection + a short reservoir fine-tune.

KV per token

the budget is compress_ratios × index_topk: 2 tokens fold into one latent on the deep layers, each query may reach 512 compressed positions; storage rides fp8 (window) and fp4 (compressed) — the "1/4 HBM, 1/8 SSD" shape.

fp8

weights ride native float8 on the Ada tensor cores; the fp4 experts cast to fp8 losslessly (zero information lost, no fp4 kernel). The >200 GB ngram shards stay on the U.2 lake, row-sharded per rank.
